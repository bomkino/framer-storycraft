# House method

Use this method to make a Framer site feel authored rather than templated. Preserve the
method; derive the surface from the current brand.

## Contents

- Proportional depth
- Actual goal and truth
- Story and mould
- Optional pitch.dog overlay
- Non-negotiables

## Choose proportional depth

| Mode | Use when | Required proof |
|---|---|---|
| Lean | One to three routes, light CMS, no bespoke scroll system | Goal and truth brief, compact style/component mould, one golden page, responsive/accessibility/release pass |
| Standard | Multi-page brand site, reusable components, moderate CMS | Three-width Blueprint, native masters, content states, one golden route family, staged route batches |
| Cinematic | Scroll scenes, specialist code objects, heavy media, complex CMS | Full Blueprint plus runtime Story Lab, scene contracts, property snapshots, performance evidence, release manifest |

Do not use a Cinematic process to flatter a small site. Do not weaken truth,
accessibility, rollback, or publish gates in Lean mode.

## Establish the actual goal

Record:

- the real-world result the site must produce;
- the primary visitor and the decision they need to make;
- the questions the page must answer, in order;
- the evidence available for every important claim;
- the most useful next action;
- content, privacy, accessibility, performance, plan, and launch constraints;
- anything that must remain blocked instead of being invented.

Order the visitor's understanding:

1. What is this?
2. Is it for me?
3. Why should I believe it?
4. How does it work?
5. What does it cost or require?
6. What should I do next?

Make the site earn attention. Never waste it.

## Build from truth

Test every public statement for:

- truth: supported by an approved source;
- usefulness: answers a real question or advances the story;
- specificity: names an action, object, tension, or consequence;
- placement: appears where that question naturally arises;
- identity: could not be pasted unchanged into a generic competitor site.

Keep unresolved facts visibly gated. Do not disguise them with polished placeholder
copy. Keep proof before process theory and action after uncertainty is reduced.

## Stage the story

Build pages as arguments, not bags of modules:

1. Establish relevance.
2. Show strong evidence early.
3. Reveal the judgement or mechanism behind the result.
4. Explain the working relationship, requirements, or terms.
5. Resolve remaining uncertainty.
6. End with one legible decision.

Alternate proof, explanation, calm, human context, and decision. Avoid consecutive
spectacle. Make every page family structurally dependable without repeating the same
personality.

## Make the mould before multiplying pages

- Build foundations and reusable masters before cloning routes.
- Build one real golden page before formalising its page family.
- Prove viewport-dependent behaviour in Preview or a protected runtime page; a
  beautiful Design Page is not runtime evidence.
- Expand in small route-family batches and review between batches.
- Preserve the first approved composition as the reference; do not bulk-propagate an
  unresolved idea.

## Apply the optional pitch.dog house overlay

Apply only when the user wants pitch.dog's sensibility. Never copy literal colours,
fonts, dog imagery, route names, or components into an unrelated brand.

- Prefer editorial composition, tactile materiality, warmth, wit, and exactness.
- Let colour, atmosphere, selected media, or a rail reach the viewport edge; cap
  reading measure and useful object sizes.
- Use controlled asymmetry, generous negative space, disciplined type, a narrow
  palette, compact utility labels, and one structural idea per view.
- Let real work prove, illustration interpret, and photography identify.
- Use accent as punctuation rather than a page-wide wash.
- Add one human irregularity, not decorative noise.
- Let humour follow clarity.
- Reject fake urgency, conversion theatre, gatekeeper worship, manipulative funnels,
  synthetic polish, and motion wallpaper.
- Express politics materially through dignity, credit, access, consent, and honest
  terms rather than decorative virtue signals.

## Non-negotiables

- Recompose Phone around reading order and touch; never shrink Desktop.
- Author reduced motion as a complete result.
- Keep essential meaning, policy, pricing, forms, and navigation stable.
- Give one viewport one claim and one dominant motion owner.
- Let every moving sequence resolve into a stable, useful state.
- Require explicit authority before publishing.
