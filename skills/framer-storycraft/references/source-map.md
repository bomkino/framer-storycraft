## Keep universal

- Solve the visitor's decision before styling.
- Gate unsupported claims instead of inventing them.
- Build a mould before multiplying pages.
- Separate Canvas composition from runtime proof.
- Let native Framer own meaning and code own specialist mechanisms.
- Recompose Phone and author reduced motion.
- Make motion change understanding and resolve into a useful state.
- Preserve component identity instead of merging by visual similarity.
- Bound video, clocks, decoders, observers, and cleanup.
- Mutate in small reversible batches.
- Separate readiness, placement, tests, route policy, and release.
- Publish only a sanitised, reviewed, reversible candidate.

## Keep only as an optional house overlay

- Editorial, tactile, warm, exact, witty composition.
- Proof before process explanation.
- Controlled asymmetry, disciplined type, generous negative space, narrow palettes,
  and accent as punctuation.
- Stillness between theatrical peaks.
- Real work as proof, illustration as interpretation, photography as identity.
- Rejection of generic agency language, manipulative funnels, conversion theatre,
  fake urgency, synthetic polish, and ornamental politics.
- Suggested breakpoint, wrapper, spacing, type-floor, target-size, and motion defaults.

Derive literal palette, typography, image system, iconography, tone, and motion feel
from the current brand. Never make another project look like a pitch.dog clone.

## Exclude from the portable skill

Do not reproduce:

- private, pending, owner-gated, commercial, legal, identity, recognition, or project
  claims;
- exact studio facts, rates, programme rules, dates, or approval owners;
- exact public navigation, route inventory, page copy, or CMS item counts;
- exact typography and colour-token matrices;
- the complete illustration-room inventory or asset hashes;
- the complete custom component source collection or project placement map;
- purchased third-party component source or undocumented behaviour;
- raw supplied media, private screenshots, or internal evidence.

## Reverify time-sensitive Framer facts

Before relying on them, inspect the current Framer product, official documentation, or
live tool surface for:

- plan limits and CMS/page capacity;
- external-agent capabilities and permissions;
- available native Canvas controls and accessibility attributes;
- responsive image/video delivery;
- reduced-motion behaviour;
- Layout Template, overflow, sticky, and Fixed behaviour;
- third-party Lenis/Revelo versions, properties, DOM output, and cleanup.

Treat dated vendor claims as test hypotheses, not current guarantees.
